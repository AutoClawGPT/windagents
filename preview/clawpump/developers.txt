The pair reference explains fee limits, payout assets and quote consistency. Your key A key is a single opaque string, cpk_ plus 43 base64url characters. ClawPump stores only a SHA-256 hash of the key plus its first 12 characters, so a lost key cannot be recovered and must be reissued. The key is bound to one ClawPump account. Every agent, wallet, and credit balance reached through the key belongs to that account. Treat it as a bearer secret: server-side only, never in browser or mobile bundles. Enterprise keys are issued by ClawPump. Rotation and revocation go through dev@clawpump.tech . Deactivation is immediate, but key lookups are cached for 60 seconds at each of two layers, so allow up to two minutes for a revoked key to stop working everywhere.
